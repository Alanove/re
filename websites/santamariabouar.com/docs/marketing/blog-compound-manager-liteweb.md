If you run a compound or condo, you already know the mess:

- Common charges tracked in Excel
- Payment reminders lost in WhatsApp groups

Paper files pile up. Messages get deleted. And when someone leaves or a guest stays longer than planned, the gate team is the last to know.

## Transactions that stay clear

Common charges are the heart of compound life. Compound Manager helps you create and follow them without drama.

Build yearly charges based on apartment size. See what is unpaid, partially paid, or fully paid. Record cash or bank payments. Print receipts. Run reports when the board asks for numbers.

**What you get:**

- Common expense invoices by year
- Payment status you can read at a glance
- Receipts and payment history
- Reports for unpaid dues and credits

Money talks get shorter when the numbers are in one system.

## Wallets for each apartment

Some compounds keep a prepaid balance for each unit. Compound Manager supports that with **wallets**.

Add or adjust wallet credit. Use wallet funds toward invoices. Let residents see their balance and history in the portal.

**What you get:**

- One wallet per apartment
- Clear credit and debit history
- Pay dues from wallet (or mix with cash/bank)
- Less chasing for small balances

It is simple bookkeeping — built for real compound life.

## Gate access and guests — one living list

The main gate needs a list they can trust.

With Compound Manager, each apartment has authorized people: owners, tenants, family, staff, guests, and more. Add an end date when access should expire. Residents can request changes. Admins approve. The gate list stays up to date.

**What you get:**

- Authorized people per apartment
- Guest and family access with optional expiry
- Admin approval for add or remove requests
- Search and filter by block, unit, or name

This is not a hardware turnstile app. It is the living list your compound actually uses — clear, shared, and easy to update.

## Rent and sale — right on your compound site

Owners often want to rent or sell. Spreading ads on random pages creates noise and confusion.

Compound Manager lets residents submit a **for rent** or **for sale** listing request. Admins review price, photos, and details. When approved, the listing can appear on your compound’s public pages — clean, branded, and under your control.

**What you get:**

- For rent and for sale listings
- Photo review before publish
- Clear details: price, beds, amenities, contact options
- Inquire forms for people who want to visit or ask

Your compound becomes easier to market — without losing control.
