Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline? expand_more
Timelines vary based on complexity. A standard MVP typically takes 8-12 weeks, while enterprise-grade custom software can range from 4 to 9 months. We provide a detailed project schedule during the strategy phase.
Do you offer post-launch support? expand_more
Yes, we provide several maintenance tiers that include security updates, server monitoring, performance optimization, and ongoing feature development.
Are hosting and domain included? expand_more
Hosting and domain are a separate yearly payment, not included in the project build fee. We can recommend and set up a plan that fits your traffic and budget, then renew it with you each year.
Can you set up professional email? expand_more
Yes. We work with you to choose the right professional email service — Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or another provider — based on your team size, needs, and budget, then help you get it running on your domain.
Do you help with social media setup and tracking? expand_more
Yes. We help set up business pages and install tracking (pixels and analytics) so you can measure campaigns, ads, and site activity from day one.

Tell us what you need built

A website, a shop, or the system that currently lives in spreadsheets. Send a note and an engineer will reply.
