Sound familiar?

If you sell properties or vehicles, the listing site is often the weakest part of the business:

  • inventory_2 Inventory lives in spreadsheets, then goes stale on the website
  • map Buyers cannot search by map, radius, or neighborhood
  • chat Inquiries land in WhatsApp and never reach the right agent
  • swap_horiz One site that cannot switch between houses and cars
  • filter_alt Filters that ignore trim, mileage, beds, or square footage
  • hourglass_empty Hot leads go cold because nobody followed up in time

Your stock changes every day. Buyers expect a live catalog, a map, and a reply now — not a contact form that sits unread.

There is a better way.

One engine, two marketplace UIs

LiteWeb builds a listing platform around a single inventory, search, and lead model. The taxonomy switches the interface: property cards for real estate, vehicle cards for automotive. Same engine. The right fields, filters, and maps for each vertical.

You tell us how your stock is structured. We build the catalog, the search, and the routing so agents and dealers work from one system.

Real estate listing platform

Agencies and developers need more than a photo and a price. Each property has a type, a location, a set of rooms, and an agent who should see the inquiry first. The real estate UI is built for that.

What you get:

  • check_circle Property types: apartment, villa, land, commercial, and new developments
  • check_circle Beds, baths, square footage, floor, parking, and furnishing as first-class filters
  • check_circle Map pin, neighborhood, and radius search so buyers browse by location
  • check_circle Viewing requests routed to the listing agent with name, number, and preferred time

Listings stay tied to the agent or office that owns them. When stock changes, the public card updates with it.

Automotive listing platform

Dealerships sell by make, model, trim, and condition — not by neighborhood. The automotive UI uses vehicle taxonomy so buyers can filter the lot the way they shop in person.

What you get:

  • check_circle Make, model, year, trim, mileage, fuel, and body style as searchable fields
  • check_circle New, used, and certified stock with VIN-style inventory codes your team already uses
  • check_circle Dealer lot location on the map, plus filters for price, drivetrain, and features
  • check_circle Test-drive and price inquiries scored and routed to the right salesperson

When a car is sold or reserved, it leaves the public catalog. Your team is not chasing leads on vehicles that are already gone.

Maps buyers actually use

A listing page without a map is a catalog. Buyers want to draw an area, set a radius, and see what is for sale around a school, a highway, or a showroom.

What you get:

  • check_circle Custom polygon drawing so buyers search a neighborhood, not a city name
  • check_circle Radius search and point-of-interest overlays
  • check_circle Vector clustering that stays smooth with thousands of pins
  • check_circle Location APIs so traffic, commute, and nearby amenities can sit on the same map

How we build it

We start from your inventory, not a generic template. Search, maps, and leads sit on top of a model that matches how you actually sell.

1

Inventory model

Property or vehicle fields, photos, status, and who owns each listing. The public card is generated from that record.

2
