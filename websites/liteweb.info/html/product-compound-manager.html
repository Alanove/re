Compound Manager by LiteWeb

Stop running your compound on WhatsApp and Excel

One simple system for apartments, money, gate access, and rent or sale listings.

Start a Project See a live compound site

Managing a compound should not feel like a second job

If you run a compound or condo, you already know the mess:

  • table_chart Common charges tracked in Excel
  • chat Payment reminders lost in WhatsApp groups
  • door_front Gate lists that nobody updates
  • help Owners asking “who lives in this apartment?” with no clear answer
  • campaign Rent and sale ads posted everywhere — and nowhere official

Paper files pile up. Messages get deleted. And when someone leaves or a guest stays longer than planned, the gate team is the last to know.

There is a better way.

Meet Compound Manager by LiteWeb

Compound Manager is a compound / condo management platform built by LiteWeb. It brings the daily work of your compound into one place.

Managers get a clear admin view. Owners and residents get a simple portal. Your compound website can even show approved listings to people looking to rent or buy.

Apartments under control

Every unit has a home in the system. See apartments by block and number. Track who owns or rents each unit. Know what is vacant, occupied, for rent, or for sale. Keep notes, photos, and history in one file — not five folders.

What you get:

  • check_circle Clear apartment registry for the whole compound
  • check_circle Owners and tenants linked to each unit
  • check_circle Status you can trust (owned, rented, vacant, listed)
  • check_circle Unit statements when you need them

No more guessing. No more “I’ll check and message you later.”

Transactions that stay clear

Common charges are the heart of compound life. Compound Manager helps you create and follow them without drama. Build yearly charges based on apartment size. See what is unpaid, partially paid, or fully paid. Record cash or bank payments. Print receipts. Run reports when the board asks for numbers.

What you get:

  • check_circle Common expense invoices by year
  • check_circle Payment status you can read at a glance
  • check_circle Receipts and payment history
  • check_circle Reports for unpaid dues and credits

Money talks get shorter when the numbers are in one system.

Wallets for each apartment

Some compounds keep a prepaid balance for each unit. Compound Manager supports that with wallets. Add or adjust wallet credit. Use wallet funds toward invoices. Let residents see their balance and history in the portal.

What you get:

  • check_circle One wallet per apartment
  • check_circle Clear credit and debit history
  • check_circle Pay dues from wallet (or mix with cash/bank)
  • check_circle Less chasing for small balances

It is simple bookkeeping — built for real compound life.

Gate access and guests — one living list

The main gate needs a list they can trust. With Compound Manager, each apartment has authorized people: owners, tenants, family, staff, guests, and more. Add an end date when access should expire. Residents can request changes. Admins approve. The gate list stays up to date.

What you get:

  • check_circle Authorized people per apartment
  • check_circle Guest and family access with optional expiry
  • check_circle Admin approval for add or remove requests
  • check_circle Search and filter by block, unit, or name

This is not a hardware turnstile app. It is the living list your compound actually uses — clear, shared, and easy to update.

Rent and sale — right on your compound site

Owners often want to rent or sell. Spreading ads on random pages creates noise and confusion. Compound Manager lets residents submit a for rent or for sale listing request. Admins review price, photos, and details. When approved, the listing can appear on your compound’s public pages — clean, branded, and under your control.

What you get:

  • check_circle For rent and for sale listings
  • check_circle Photo review before publish
  • check_circle Clear details: price, beds, amenities, contact options
  • check_circle Inquire forms for people who want to visit or ask

Your compound becomes easier to market — without losing control.
