A website alone is not always enough

If your customers live on their phones, you already know the friction:

  • language Important actions buried in a mobile browser that feels like a desktop site
  • notifications_off No reliable way to reach people when an order, booking, or status changes
  • chat Staff still closing deals and support over WhatsApp with no shared system
  • devices Separate “apps” that are really bookmarks — slow, unsigned, and easy to abandon
  • sync_problem Data that never matches what the office sees on the computer

Customers expect a button on the home screen, a clear login, and the one or two jobs they came to do — fast. Anything else and they leave.

There is a better way.

Customer apps and staff apps

Sometimes the app is for your customers. Sometimes it is for field staff, drivers, or store teams. Often you need both — with different screens and the same source of truth.

What you get:

  • check_circle Consumer apps for booking, ordering, loyalty, or self-service
  • check_circle Internal apps for tasks, inventory, approvals, and checklists
  • check_circle Role-based views so staff never see the wrong data
  • check_circle Shared APIs so the office dashboard stays in sync

One engineering team. One backend. Two audiences when you need them.

Push, login, and store readiness

An app is not finished when the screens look good. It needs accounts that stay secure, notifications that arrive, and a path through App Store and Google Play review without last-minute panic.

What you get:

  • check_circle Secure auth (email, phone, or SSO when required)
  • check_circle Push notifications for the events that matter
  • check_circle Store listing, icons, and release support
  • check_circle Updates you can ship after launch without rebuilding from zero

We stay through submission — not just until the demo video.

Tied to the systems you already run

An orphan app that cannot see your inventory, bookings, or CRM becomes another silo. We connect to what you have — or build the API layer with you if it does not exist yet.
