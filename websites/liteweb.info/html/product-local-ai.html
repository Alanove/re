AI that never leaves your building

Public AI is powerful — and the wrong place for secrets

If you run a law firm, clinic, finance team, or any office with confidential files, you already know the tension:

  • cloud_off Staff paste client text into public chat tools because it is fast
  • gavel Policies say “no confidential uploads” — but nobody has a safe alternative
  • folder_zip Knowledge lives in PDFs, contracts, and shared drives no model has read
  • psychology_alt Generic AI answers that miss how your firm actually works
  • warning One mistaken paste can put privileged material on someone else’s servers

You want the speed of AI. You also need the documents to stay where they belong — on hardware you control.

There is a better way.

Meet Local AI Setup by LiteWeb

Local AI Setup is a private AI environment built by LiteWeb. Models and indexes run on your premises or private network. Your team asks questions in plain language. Answers come from your files — not from the open internet.

A lawyer finds the clause from last year’s matter in seconds. A partner drafts from firm precedents without uploading the file to a stranger’s cloud. IT keeps the keys.

Train on how your firm actually works

A law firm is the clearest example: matter files, templates, opinions, and internal how-to notes. We help you select what to index, clean the junk, and connect the model so answers cite the sources your lawyers trust.

What you get:

  • check_circle Ingestion of PDFs, Word docs, email exports, and shared folders
  • check_circle Matter-aware or department-aware collections
  • check_circle Answers grounded in your corpus, with source links when useful
  • check_circle Ongoing refresh when new documents land

The same pattern works for clinics, accounting firms, manufacturers, and any team whose knowledge is locked in files.

Secret documents can stay in the loop

Public chat tools are off-limits for privileged material. Local AI flips that rule: because inference runs on your hardware, confidential contracts, HR files, and sealed exhibits can be part of the knowledge base under your access policy.

What you get:

  • check_circle No document upload to third-party model providers for day-to-day use
  • check_circle Network boundaries you define (office LAN, VPN, or air-gapped options)
  • check_circle Audit logs for who queried what and when
  • check_circle Clear separation between public marketing AI and private firm AI

Speed without the policy violation. That is the point.

Hardware, models, and a usable chat

We size the box (or private cloud) to your load, install the model stack, and give your team a simple chat or search UI. You should not need a research lab to ask “what did we say about X last year?”

What you get:

  • check_circle Hardware or private-cloud sizing that matches your document volume
  • check_circle Model selection for your languages and privacy constraints
  • check_circle A chat or portal your staff can use on day one
  • check_circle Admin tools to add collections and revoke access

We set it up, train your admins, and stay for the first months of tuning.

Policies, roles, and safe daily use

Technology alone does not fix risk. We help you define who can see which collections, what must never be exported, and how Local AI sits next to the public tools people still use for non-sensitive work.

What you get:

  • check_circle Role-based access by practice group or department
  • check_circle Retention and deletion aligned with your compliance needs
  • check_circle Staff guidance so the private tool becomes the default for firm files
  • check_circle Optional tie-in to LiteWeb AI Agents for external customers — without mixing corpora

Public agents for customers. Local AI for the firm’s own brain. Separate on purpose.
