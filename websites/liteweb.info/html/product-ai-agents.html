AI Agents by LiteWeb

Never miss another customer call

An AI agent is a digital staff member. We train it on how your company talks — hours, prices, services, and rules. It then answers people on the phone, WhatsApp, or your website, day and night. When it is unsure, it hands the conversation to a real person.

Start a Project Schedule a Demo

Sound familiar?

If you run a shop, clinic, or service business, you already know the mess:

  • call_missed Missed calls after hours — and the customer never calls back
  • replay The same questions every day: hours, price, location, “are you open?”
  • hourglass_empty Leads that go cold because nobody followed up
  • chat Staff stuck answering the same WhatsApp messages
  • event Appointments booked by hand, then double-booked

Your team is busy doing the real work. The phone and chat never stop. Customers expect an answer now — not tomorrow morning.

There is a better way.

What is an AI agent?

Think of it as a teammate who never sleeps. It talks like your business. It knows your hours, your services, and your prices. It can take a message, book a time, or pass the person to you when the question is too specific.

You do not need to be a software engineer. You tell us how you want customers treated. We build the agent to match.

Custom phone calls

This is the agent most companies ask for first. A customer calls your number. The agent picks up in your company name, in the language you choose, and follows the script you approve.

What a caller hears:

  • check_circle A greeting that sounds like your business, not a robot menu
  • check_circle Answers to hours, location, prices, and services
  • check_circle Qualification: who they are, what they need, when to call back
  • check_circle Booking a visit, or a clear message for your team

If the caller needs a person, the agent transfers or leaves a summary so you are not starting from zero.

Website greeter

How it works

You stay in control. The agent does the repetitive talking. Your people handle the exceptions.

1

We learn your business

Hours, services, prices, common questions, and how you like to speak to customers.

2

We connect the channels

Phone, WhatsApp, your website — or all three. Callers and chatters reach the same trained agent.

3

Your team stays in control

When the agent is unsure, it passes the person to you with a short summary. You can change the answers anytime.

Who AI Agents are for
