Who we are

A pioneer of independent consolidation in Lebanon, trusted by Beirut's freight forwarders for total neutrality.

Founded in May 1998, OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl manages consolidation import services from the world's major ports and a worldwide LCL export service with our Antwerp partners, SSC Consolidation. Our staff supervise every container's receipt and deconsolidation at Beirut port, in cooperation with the port and customs authorities.

Services

Six ways we move your cargo.

  1. LCL co-loading at competitive rates for Beirut forwarders, plus a dedicated FCL department. Weekly direct services with "no fail" schedules on CMA CGM, Maersk, MSC and Hamburg Süd.

  2. Worldwide LCL export to more than 2,165 destinations through Antwerp, with weekly services to New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, Charleston and Houston, and tracking until the consignee collects.

  3. A full range of air logistics, planned and measured to Cargo 2000 procedures and monitored door to door, with out-of-hours delivery for perishable cargo and project shipments.

  4. Beirut is one of the fastest transshipment platforms in the Mediterranean. Weekly consolidation to Lattakia in one day, and FCL transshipment to Iraq.

  5. International household moves door to door through the HHGFAA network: pick-up and delivery, outside cranes, storage, and dismantling and assembly of furniture.

  6. A customs-approved warehouse inside Beirut port's secured area, including EURO ZONE, reefer storage and one delivery order for multiple origins.
