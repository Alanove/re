About Oceanair

One of Lebanon's largest neutral NVOCCs, since 1998.

Since May 1998, OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl has built its name on careful cargo handling and refined service. Our management's experience in consolidation goes back more than two decades, which makes us one of the pioneers of independent consolidation in Lebanon.

As a member of the WorldWide Alliance we run 12 weekly direct import services and a worldwide export consolidation service with our Antwerp partners, SSC Consolidation.

  • Total neutrality for Beirut freight forwarders
  • "No fail" sailing schedules on premium carriers
  • Staff on site at Beirut port receiving depots
  • First class service from inquiry to final delivery
