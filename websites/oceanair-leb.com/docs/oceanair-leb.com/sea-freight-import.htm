Sea Freight Import

LCL Service (less than container load)

Managing consolidation import services from major world wide ports serving the need of BEIRUT local freight forwarders to whom “OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” is offering co-loading competitive rates, reliable service and total neutrality.

Thanks to a network of worldwide neutral NVOCC agents we gain the respect consequently the support of major global logistics service providers.

Our weekly import direct services and our “”NO FAIL”” sailing schedules using premium carriers such as CMA-CGM, Maersk, MSC and Hamburg sud made of “OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” one of the most trusted consolidators in Lebanon.

A team of 4 persons is in control of Beirut port receiving depots. in cooperation with the port and custom authority our staff is physically supervising the receipt and deconsolidation operations of our import containers.

“OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” is ready to do its utmost to provide customers with efficient service and at the same time fulfill its social responsibility as a leading NVOCC firm in LEBANON.


FCL Service (full container load)

The growing demand to offer FCL service has encouraged us to create a department fully dedicated to this type of transportation which, initially, was not part of our range of services.

Our sales policy and our network of agents have made of our FCL department a success.
