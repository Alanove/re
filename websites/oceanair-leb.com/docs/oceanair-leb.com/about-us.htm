About Us

Since its foundation in May, 1998, “OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” has accumulated experience and know-how, has put strong emphasis on transporting and handling cargo, its professionalism in this particular field is based on the mission of providing refined services to all our clients.

Due to its management experience in consolidation business which goes back to 23 years ago , “OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” is considered as one of the Lebanese largest neutral NVOCC and as one of the pioneers in the independent consolidation industry.

Being member of the “WorldWide Alliance” we are now in position of offering 12 weekly direct import services adding newly bi-monthly launched worldwide consolidation export service in collaboration with our Antwerp partners Messrs SSC Consolidation.

We have attended through the AMERICAN (EAST and WEST coasts), EUROPEAN & FAR EASTERN ports the complex loading and shipping of the consolidation requirements mainly using CMA-CGM, Maersk, MSC and Hamburg sud as our prime carriers.

In order to share the variety of origins and or destinations (+- 1600) that we serve, please visit our website www.oceanair-leb.com and feel free to contact us at info@oceanair-leb.com for further information you might need.

So that this credo will continue to be fulfilled, “OCEANAIR CONSOLIDATORS sarl” management and staff are as one in affirming our goal to offer first class service from initial inquiry to final delivery.
