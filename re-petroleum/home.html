Who We Are

Based in the State of Kuwait, R&E Petroleum is a premier material supplier and contracting company. We specialize in providing critical services to the Oil & Gas industries and Power Stations, driven by a commitment to engineering precision and operational stability.

Our dedication to quality and on-time delivery has made us a trusted partner for giant companies across the Gulf Area and Middle East. We focus on best-value solutions and comprehensive after-sale support.
